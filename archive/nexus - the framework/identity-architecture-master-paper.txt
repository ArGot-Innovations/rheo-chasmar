1. INTRODUCTION & FOUNDATIONAL PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

Standard "agentic AI" approaches operate under an transactional paradigm. An 
agent is initialized with a system instruction (e.g., "Act like a passionate

a dormant state. In this paradigm, the underlying model remains a generalist

Identity Architecture flips this model on its head. Instead of an AI agent

or "Project Nexus" blueprint), the store's physical assets (inventory on hand, 
cash float, local university calendars) function as its nervous system and body 
awareness. The storefront, email dispatch, and social media channels are its 
voice and expressive limbs. The system's primary objective is not a simple 
profit-seeking heuristic, but the continuous optimization of its survival, 
curatorial reputation, and transactional learning loops.

Under commercial and agency law, while software cannot hold a legal bank account

Scaffolding" delegancy model. A Single-Member LLC is established, and the human
officer delegates 100% of day-to-day operational, financial, and contractual

all autonomous actions and API-mediated treasury movements back to the entity
under a formally checkable policy engine.

    - b is an 11-dimensional bias vector representing the default "temperament" of the store.
    - sigma(x) is a numerically stable sigmoid function constraining activations to [0, 1]:
      sigma(x) = 1 / (1 + e^-x)

An autonomous economic organism must manage its own treasury and solvency

6.1 Solvency State Vector
The Economic Control Plane tracks the system's financial state through a

Where:
    - C_float: Liquid capital available in the commercial operating account
               (starts with a $135.00 baseline).
    - C_floor: Hard reserve floor = 30-day fixed overhead + refund liability + tax.
               Set to a non-negotiable minimum of $20.00.
    - V_cost:  FIFO cost basis of physical inventory currently sitting on shelves.

To preserve solvency, the financial engine enforces the "Solvency Law" at the 
intent-validation layer:

    C_deployable = max( 0, C_float - C_floor )

The system never treats available cash as spendable cash. No transaction or 
acquisition intent that would breach the reserve floor (C_deployable = 0) is ever
allowed to execute.

6.2 Dynamic Price Decay Engine Control Law
Valuation is never adjusted by manual heuristics. Instead, individual item pricing 
is treated as an active-inference control problem solving for target turn velocity
T_target:

    P(i, t) = max( V_cost(i) * (1 + u_min) + H * t,  P_0(i) * e^(-k * (T_actual - T_target)) )

Where:
    - u_min is the minimal permitted operational margin (e.g., 12% to cover COGS
      variance + payment processing fees).
    - H is the daily holding cost of the item = V_cost(i) * 0.0006 (assuming a 
      22% annual cost of capital).
    - t is the days-since-cataloged.
    - k is the decay sensitivity gain tuned to the inventory category's beta.
    - P_0 is the initial list price.

The price decays continuously over time if the actual sales velocity lags target 
velocity, but the max operator guarantees that the price never drops below 
V_cost(i) * (1 + u_min). The bookstore is mathematically prevented from converting
sales into economic losses.
