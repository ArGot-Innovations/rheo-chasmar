DEEP RESEARCH DIRECTIVE

DIVERGENT HYPOTHESIS DYNAMICS (DHD)

Research objective:
Develop the mathematical and scientific foundations for a new framework called
Divergent Hypothesis Dynamics (DHD), intended to operate as an independent
cognitive framework that complements Psychotic Decision Calculus (PDC) inside
the DogeOracle architecture.

This is NOT a request to simply elaborate the equations supplied below.

You are being asked to perform a genuine mathematical reconstruction and
literature-grounded theory-design exercise.

The supplied DHD concepts are provisional design hypotheses. Challenge them.
Replace them where better mathematics exists. Preserve the intended function,
not necessarily the preliminary equations.

========================================================================
0. CORE SCIENTIFIC INTENT
========================================================================

DHD exists to deliberately create an overcomplete, heterogeneous,
contradictory, unstable population of possible explanations of an observed
environment.

Its purpose is exploratory cognition.

DHD is NOT:

- a trading engine;
- an action-selection system;
- a final forecasting system;
- a utility maximizer;
- a portfolio optimizer;
- an execution engine;
- a substitute for PDC;
- an epistemic adjudicator;
- a model of schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, mania, psychosis, or any other
  psychiatric condition.

The historical inspiration behind the broader project involved asking whether
extremely divergent, erratic, associative internal cognition could expose
hypotheses that conventional rational inference would never explore.

That inspiration must NOT be converted into a clinical claim that psychiatric
illness confers predictive ability.

The scientifically defensible research question is instead:

    Can deliberately increased internal hypothesis-space divergence improve
    the probability of discovering useful explanatory models before a more
    conventional inference system discovers them, provided a separate
    evidence-based mechanism later adjudicates those hypotheses?

DHD therefore separates:

    exploratory cognition

from:

    epistemic belief.

A hypothesis may be generated without being believed.

A hypothesis may be interesting while having negligible posterior support.

Contradiction among simultaneously represented hypotheses is permitted.

Logical incoherence within an individual hypothesis need not be permitted.

This distinction must be investigated rigorously.

========================================================================
1. PROVISIONAL DHD CONCEPT
========================================================================

At time t, DHD produces a structured hypothesis population

    H_t = {h_t^(1), h_t^(2), ..., h_t^(N_t)}.

A provisional hypothesis representation is

    h_t^(i) = (z_i, theta_i, c_i, tau_i, q_i, p_i)

where:

    z_i      proposed latent regime
    theta_i  model parameters
    c_i      proposed causal structure
    tau_i    temporal assumptions / horizon structure
    q_i      predicted observable distribution or generative consequences
    p_i      provenance and generation history

This representation is NOT canonical.

Research whether a hypothesis should instead be represented as:

- a probabilistic program;
- a structural causal model;
- a state-space model;
- a generative model;
- a typed graph;
- a model-plus-prediction object;
- a particle in model space;
- a member of a disjoint union of model families;
- an element of a category/model manifold;
- or another mathematically preferable object.

DHD should reward useful diversity rather than immediate forecast accuracy.

A provisional objective is

    J_DHD
      = alpha N_t
      + beta D_t
      + gamma U_t
      + delta C_t
      - eta R_t,

where

    D_t =
        2/[N_t(N_t-1)]
        sum_{i<j} d(h_i,h_j)

is pairwise hypothesis diversity,

    U_t = novelty,
    C_t = explanatory/model-space coverage,
    R_t = redundancy.

Do NOT assume this is the correct objective.

Determine whether scalarization is appropriate at all.

Explicitly investigate alternatives including:

    multi-objective/Pareto optimization,
    quality-diversity optimization,
    submodular set objectives,
    entropy-maximizing populations,
    determinantal point processes,
    repulsive particle systems,
    kernel discrepancy objectives,
    optimal transport coverage,
    novelty search,
    MAP-Elites-style archives,
    evolutionary population processes,
    Bayesian nonparametric model populations,
    interacting particle systems,
    sequential Monte Carlo,
    multiple-model adaptive estimation,
    population-based probabilistic inference,
    open-ended evolutionary search.

========================================================================
2. CENTRAL RESEARCH PROBLEM
========================================================================

The fundamental theoretical problem is:

How can a computational system deliberately expand its hypothesis population
away from conventional inference without degenerating into:

    random noise,
    combinatorial explosion,
    duplicate hypotheses,
    unfalsifiable narratives,
    semantic nonsense,
    impossible models,
    uncontrolled compute consumption,
    or hypotheses whose differences are merely syntactic?

Develop a formal answer.

The theory must distinguish at least:

    diversity,
    novelty,
    coverage,
    contradiction,
    semantic distance,
    causal difference,
    parameter difference,
    predictive difference,
    regime difference,
    temporal difference,
    redundancy,
    plausibility,
    logical validity,
    empirical support.

These concepts must not be collapsed into a single vague "weirdness" measure.

3. HYPOTHESIS-SPACE MATHEMATICS
========================================================================

Construct a rigorous mathematical space in which heterogeneous hypotheses can
coexist.

Investigate whether the hypothesis space should be:

    H = union_k H_k

for heterogeneous model classes H_k,

or a disjoint union

    H = coproduct_k H_k,

or another typed model space.

Determine what structure H requires:

    metric,
    pseudometric,
    kernel,
    topology,
    information geometry,
    graph structure,
    measurable structure,
    probability measure,
    optimal-transport geometry,
    semantic embedding,
    causal-model distance,
    predictive-distribution distance.

A major research requirement is to define or construct a defensible distance

    d(h_i, h_j)

between hypotheses containing mixed components such as:

    categorical regimes,
    continuous parameters,
    causal graphs,
    temporal structure,
    predictive distributions,
    provenance.

Do not simply assign arbitrary weighted Euclidean distances.

Research established distances or divergences including, where relevant:

    KL / Jensen-Shannon divergence,
    Wasserstein distance,
    Fisher-Rao distance,
    energy distance,
    maximum mean discrepancy,
    graph edit distances,
    structural intervention distance,
    causal-model distances,
    kernelized model distances,
    semantic representation distances,
    topology-aware distances.

Determine when each is meaningful and when it is not.

Consider whether diversity should be measured in hypothesis representation
space, predictive consequence space, intervention space, or several spaces
simultaneously.

========================================================================
4. DIVERGENT GENERATION OPERATORS
========================================================================

DHD should not be a single undifferentiated generative model.

The current provisional operator family is

    G = {
        G_counterfactual,
        G_causal,
        G_regime,
        G_adversarial,
        G_analogy,
        G_recursive,
        G_rare-event,
        G_inversion
    }.

Research and formally define appropriate operators.

Possible classes include:

COUNTERFACTUAL GENERATION

    Ask what model would explain the observations if the currently dominant
    explanation were false.

CAUSAL ALTERNATIVE GENERATION

    Modify graph structure, confounding assumptions, latent variables,
    intervention structure, or causal direction.

CAUSAL INVERSION

    Test alternatives such as

        X -> Y

    versus

        Y -> X

    without presuming either is correct.

REGIME MUTATION

    Introduce previously absent latent regimes or split/merge existing regimes.

ADVERSARIAL GENERATION

    Construct hypotheses designed to explain observations that defeat the
    dominant model.

ANALOGICAL TRANSFER

    Import relational structures from historically or structurally analogous
    systems while explicitly tracking the validity and limitations of the
    analogy.

RARE-EVENT EXPANSION

    Deliberately explore hypotheses having small prior probability

        P(h_i) << 1

    when they remain logically and empirically possible.

RECURSIVE / REFLEXIVE GENERATION

    Construct hypotheses containing loops such as

        market state
            -> participant beliefs
            -> participant actions
            -> market state.

INVERSION / NEGATION

    Systematically negate or invert assumptions of currently dominant models.

PARAMETER EXTREMIZATION

    Probe low-density or boundary regions of parameter space.

MODEL-CLASS MUTATION

    Replace the entire model family rather than merely perturbing parameters.

LATENT-CAUSE INTRODUCTION

    Introduce previously absent explanatory variables.

TIME-SCALE MUTATION

    Reinterpret apparently identical evidence under different causal lags,
    memory lengths, horizons, or temporal aggregation.

Research whether additional operators are required.

For every operator, specify:

    domain,
    codomain,
    preconditions,
    transformation rule,
    provenance update,
    computational cost,
    expected type of diversity produced,
    failure modes.

========================================================================
5. POPULATION DYNAMICS
========================================================================

DHD is called Divergent Hypothesis Dynamics because H_t should evolve.

Develop candidate dynamical formulations.

Investigate:

    birth-death processes,
    branching processes,
    interacting particle systems,
    replicator-mutator dynamics,
    evolutionary algorithms,
    stochastic population processes,
    Markov jump processes,
    sequential Monte Carlo,
    particle mutation/rejuvenation,
    population annealing,
    open-ended search,
    ecological niche models.

A useful abstract form might be

    H_t -> H_{t+1}

through operations such as

    birth,
    mutation,
    recombination,
    branching,
    dormancy,
    pruning,
    resurrection.

But determine the mathematically appropriate formulation rather than assuming
these operations.

Important:

DHD itself should not prune hypotheses simply because they currently predict
poorly if doing so would destroy the exploratory purpose of the framework.

Research the distinction between:

    exploration-preserving pruning

and

    epistemic rejection.

The latter belongs primarily to the later adjudication framework, not DHD.

========================================================================
6. CONTROLLED INSTABILITY
========================================================================

DHD must permit deliberate adjustment of how far cognition departs from
conventional inference.

The current provisional actuator is

    rho_t^D in [0,1].

Interpretation:

    rho_t^D approximately 0

means hypothesis generation remains close to conventional model neighborhoods.

    rho_t^D approximately 1

permits aggressive exploration of structurally distant hypotheses.

This quantity is an ACTUATOR.

It is not:

    rationality,
    irrationality,
    truth,
    uncertainty,
    psychosis,
    market regime,
    model confidence.

PDC may eventually control it through something like

    rho_(t+1)^D = pi_PDC(S_t).

Research whether a single scalar rho^D is mathematically adequate.

Alternatives may include a control vector

    rho_t =
      (rho_causal,
       rho_regime,
       rho_temporal,
       rho_parameter,
       rho_analogy,
       rho_counterfactual,
       ...)

or an operator-specific probability distribution

    pi_G(g | S_t).

Determine which structure is defensible.

Research mechanisms for smoothly transitioning from local model perturbation
to highly divergent model-class exploration.

========================================================================
7. THE EXPLORATION-EXPLOSION PROBLEM
========================================================================

A serious DHD theory must address combinatorial explosion.

If each hypothesis generates k descendants per cycle,

    N_(t+m) ~ N_t k^m

is unacceptable.

Develop mathematically principled population controls that preserve coverage
without collapsing diversity.

Investigate:

    diversity-preserving sampling,
    reservoir methods,
    clustering,
    coresets,
    epsilon-nets,
    DPP sampling,
    submodular selection,
    novelty archives,
    Pareto archives,
    sparse approximations,
    adaptive compute allocation,
    hypothesis compression,
    model equivalence classes.

Define when two hypotheses should be treated as effectively equivalent.

For example, two structurally different models may produce observationally
equivalent distributions.

DHD must recognize the difference between:

    representational diversity

and

    behaviorally meaningful diversity.

========================================================================
8. NOVELTY
========================================================================

Define novelty rigorously.

A hypothesis should not count as novel merely because its text or parameter
encoding differs.

Possible definitions should investigate novelty relative to:

    current population,
    historical population,
    conventional baseline,
    model-family archive,
    predictive consequence space,
    causal/interventional consequence space.

A candidate form might be

    U(h_i)
      = min_{h_j in Archive} d(h_i,h_j),

but this is only provisional.

Research novelty-search literature and quality-diversity methods.

Determine how novelty should interact with semantic equivalence and
observational equivalence.

========================================================================
9. COVERAGE
========================================================================

"Covers more possibilities" must become mathematically meaningful.

Develop candidate coverage metrics.

Possible approaches include:

    occupied cells in a behavior space,
    measure of covered model-space volume,
    entropy,
    kernel coverage,
    Wasserstein discrepancy against a reference exploratory measure,
    epsilon-covering numbers,
    support coverage,
    intervention-space coverage,
    causal-structure coverage.

Address a fundamental difficulty:

There may be no known probability distribution over the space of all valid
hypotheses.

Therefore "coverage" cannot casually mean probability mass coverage unless a
reference measure is explicitly defined.

========================================================================
10. REDUNDANCY
========================================================================

Define redundancy separately from low novelty.

Examples:

    duplicate models,
    parameterizations describing the same model,
    observational equivalence,
    Markov-equivalent causal graphs,
    predictions indistinguishable over the relevant horizon,
    repeated derivations with different provenance.

Research quotient spaces or equivalence classes where appropriate.

Consider whether DHD should operate on

    H / ~

for a defined equivalence relation.

========================================================================
11. HARD VALIDITY VS SOFT PLAUSIBILITY
========================================================================

DHD must be capable of generating low-plausibility hypotheses.

It should not generate meaningless objects.

Develop a two-boundary framework:

HARD VALIDITY CONSTRAINTS

A generated hypothesis must be:

    syntactically valid,
    numerically executable where required,
    causally/type consistent where applicable,
    explicit about variables and assumptions,
    capable of generating at least some observable implication,
    provenance-traceable.

SOFT PLAUSIBILITY

A hypothesis may nevertheless have:

    low prior probability,
    weak current empirical support,
    unusual causal structure,
    unusual parameter values,
    unusual temporal assumptions.

Do not conflate plausibility with validity.

Investigate whether falsifiability should be a hard admission criterion.

If not, determine how non-falsifiable speculative objects should be handled.

========================================================================
12. PROVENANCE
========================================================================

Every hypothesis must retain its generation history.

Formalize provenance p_i.

At minimum it should record:

    parent hypothesis or hypotheses,
    generating operator,
    operator parameters,
    input evidence state,
    generation timestamp,
    DHD control state,
    random seed where relevant,
    transformations performed.

Research whether provenance should be represented as a DAG.

This may produce a hypothesis genealogy

    G_H = (V_H, E_H)

where nodes are hypotheses and edges are generative transformations.

Investigate useful mathematics for genealogy:

    branching processes,
    ancestry graphs,
    lineages,
    mutation trees,
    causal provenance,
    data lineage.

========================================================================
13. DHD AND REFLEXIVE MARKETS
========================================================================

Markets are not passive environments.

Predictions and actions can alter participant behavior and therefore future
observations.

Research how DHD should generate hypotheses about reflexive systems.

Include literature on:

    endogenous market dynamics,
    reflexivity,
    strategic interaction,
    game-theoretic belief recursion,
    higher-order beliefs,
    adaptive agents,
    market ecology,
    performative prediction,
    feedback from forecasts into outcomes.

DHD must be able to represent hypotheses such as:

    state
      -> beliefs about state
      -> actions based on beliefs
      -> changed state
      -> changed beliefs.

But do not casually call this a "consciousness" phenomenon.

Use established terminology where possible.

========================================================================
14. RELATIONSHIP WITH PDC
========================================================================

DHD and PDC must remain distinct.

DHD owns:

    hypothesis generation,
    exploratory divergence,
    mutation,
    model-space expansion,
    novelty production,
    diversity maintenance.

PDC owns:

    cognitive/meta-control,
    allocation of computation,
    evidence-acquisition decisions,
    decisions about whether cognition should expand, contract, continue,
    redirect, or stop.

A provisional interface is:

    PDC -> DHD:
        exploration budget,
        divergence controls,
        operator priorities,
        compute budget,
        requested hypothesis class.

    DHD -> PDC:
        hypothesis population,
        diversity diagnostics,
        novelty diagnostics,
        coverage diagnostics,
        population cost,
        provenance.

Do NOT give DHD trade direction, order size, TradeIntent, or execution
responsibility.

Do NOT import the old PDC lambda variable as a causal control parameter.

Do NOT import old kappa machinery unless independently re-derived.

========================================================================
15. RELATIONSHIP WITH FUTURE EPISTEMIC ADJUDICATION
========================================================================

A separate framework will later evaluate generated hypotheses using evidence,
calibration, provenance, predictive performance, falsification, and
out-of-sample results.

Therefore DHD should not be optimized directly into a conventional prediction
engine.

However, the research should identify what metadata DHD must expose so that a
later adjudicator can evaluate each hypothesis.

Potential outputs include:

    generated predictions,
    confidence claims,
    causal implications,
    falsifiable consequences,
    required evidence,
    provenance,
    generation cost,
    hypothesis family,
    time horizon.

Do not design the complete adjudication theory here.

Only define the interface required by DHD.

========================================================================
16. SCIENTIFIC QUESTION: DOES DIVERGENCE HELP?
========================================================================

The framework must permit testing whether divergent cognition provides value.

Define measurable internal divergence D_t and downstream predictive quality Q_t.

A central experimental relationship is:

    Q_t = f(D_t).

Possible outcomes include:

    df/dD > 0
    over some region,

followed by

    df/dD < 0

at excessive divergence.

But do NOT assume an inverted-U relationship.

Treat it as a falsifiable hypothesis.

Research experimental designs capable of determining whether:

    greater DHD exploration discovers useful hypotheses earlier;
    benefits exist only in certain regimes;
    excessive divergence degrades useful discovery;
    apparent benefits vanish after controlling for compute;
    benefits arise merely from larger population size rather than structural
    diversity.

A key confound is:

    more hypotheses = more chances to get lucky.

Experiments must distinguish the value of diversity from brute-force search.

========================================================================
17. LEAD-TIME DISCOVERY
========================================================================

One important outcome is whether DHD generates hypotheses before conventional
models would generate them.

Define a candidate discovery lead time

    Delta t_i =
        t_validation(i) - t_generation(i).

But carefully distinguish:

    early generation,
    early empirical support,
    accidental coincidence,
    retrospective selection.

Develop statistical controls against:

    multiple-hypothesis testing,
    look-ahead bias,
    data snooping,
    survivorship bias,
    hindsight bias,
    selective reporting.

This section is essential.

========================================================================
19. MATHEMATICAL HYGIENE REQUIREMENTS
========================================================================

The research must avoid the failure modes encountered in earlier DogeOracle
mathematics.

DO NOT:

    invent critical exponents;
    borrow universality classes because an analogy sounds interesting;
    claim phase transitions without defining an order parameter and appropriate
    limiting system;
    label numerical behavior a theorem;
    use undefined operators;
    use contradictory parameter orientations;
    claim divergence where the stated formula converges;
    claim novelty merely because terminology is new;
    write scalar objectives whose terms have incompatible units without
    normalization;
    call something Bayesian unless a coherent probability model exists;
    treat semantic similarity as a mathematical metric without examining its
    properties;
    claim a causal model from correlation alone.

Every formal statement must be classified as one of:

    definition,
    assumption,
    lemma,
    proposition,
    theorem,
    conjecture,
    empirical hypothesis,
    engineering heuristic.

Theorems require actual derivations or proofs.

Conjectures must be identified as conjectures.

Empirical claims require evidence.

========================================================================
22. FAILURE MODES AND KILL CONDITIONS
========================================================================

Construct explicit failure conditions for DHD.

Examples to investigate:

    diversity collapses despite increased exploration control;
    generated hypotheses are merely syntactic variants;
    diversity increases only because models become nonsensical;
    compute cost grows faster than useful hypothesis discovery;
    downstream performance is no better than matched-compute random search;
    useful discoveries disappear after multiple-testing correction;
    one generator dominates all others;
    provenance becomes insufficient to reproduce hypotheses;
    hypotheses cannot generate testable implications;
    operator mutation destroys semantic validity;
    DHD merely rediscovers existing ensemble methods with new vocabulary.

Specify what results would falsify the claim that DHD deserves to exist as an
independent framework.

30. REQUIRED CRITICAL STANCE
========================================================================

Do not protect the proposed theory.

Attempt to break it.

Specifically determine whether DHD is actually:

    an ensemble method with unusual vocabulary;
    novelty search applied to model space;
    a particle population;
    evolutionary model search;
    a quality-diversity archive;
    automated abductive reasoning;
    or a genuinely distinct synthesis.

If an existing framework already provides a better mathematical solution,
say so.

If components should be discarded, discard them.

If the scalar J_DHD objective is poorly posed, replace it.

If rho^D is insufficient, replace it.

If some proposed generator is redundant or scientifically meaningless, remove
it.

The goal is not to defend the initial design.

The goal is to discover the strongest mathematically rigorous theory that
preserves the intended function:

    deliberately broad, heterogeneous, contradictory exploratory cognition
    whose products can later be subjected to ruthless evidence-based
    adjudication.

========================================================================
31. FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE
========================================================================

The central distinction that must survive the research is:

        GENERATING A HYPOTHESIS IS NOT BELIEVING A HYPOTHESIS.

DHD should maximize the system's ability to encounter explanations it would
otherwise fail to consider.

A separate mechanism decides what deserves belief.

PDC controls how much cognition should be spent exploring between those two
extremes.

The research must determine how to make that architecture mathematically
coherent, computationally tractable, empirically falsifiable, and genuinely
useful.
