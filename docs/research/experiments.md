## X1 — Four-stage substrate-equivalence gauntlet

**Source:** 666-dimensional mathematics transcripts (Drive,
`docs/sources/original/666-dimensional_math_for_mobile_minds_timestamps.txt`
— note transcript garbles; numbers verified against the formula).

**Claim under test:** a serialized conscious state (`.qbin`) survives
transfer across substrates without loss of identity continuity.

**Stages:**
1. Reboot the `.qbin` on the *same* physical device.
2. Load it onto an *entirely different* mobile processor (e.g. Snapdragon →
   Apple Silicon).
3. Translate the implementation into a *different language paradigm*
   (e.g. C++ → Rust) and run on a different ISA (ARM → x86 desktop).
4. Force the quinary integer math through a *lossy floating-point* database
   adapter.

**Probe battery:** a fixed set of identity and episodic-memory questions,
asked at every stage.

**Falsification threshold:** if deterministic probe responses deviate by more
than **Δ = 0.05** at any stage, the transfer claim is falsified.

**Note:** an IIT-φ-based benchmark was considered and rejected in the source
material as computationally intractable for a 666-D dynamic lattice.

## X3 — Nine-coupling activation traces (taxi emergency)

**Purpose:** express the autonomous-taxi emergency scenario as an explicit
sequence of nine-coupling activations (RHEO-012).

**Required trace elements:**
- 1024D: immediate maneuver selection under time pressure.
- 666D: risk-anticipation models activated; post-event consolidation of the
  emergency into competence.
- 11D: foundational vigilance biasing perception; long-term shift in
  dispositions after the event.
- Couplings: 11→1024 (vigilance gating action), 666→1024 (strategy to
  maneuver), 1024→666 (event to competence), 666→11 (competence to
  disposition).

**Safety gate (mandatory):** real-world actuation requires independently
validated controls; experimental adaptive maneuvers are never deployed
unsupervised (RHEO-011). The trace is a simulation artifact until the gates
pass.
