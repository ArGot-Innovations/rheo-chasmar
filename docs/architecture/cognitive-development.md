# Cognitive Development in Rheo-Chasmar

**Status:** architectural intent / research draft (2026-10-10). Not a certified model.

This document records the developmental framing of the three cognitive
geometries specified in `three-geometry-cognitive-architecture.md`:
the **11-dimensional lattice** (foundational cognition), the **666-dimensional
fractal** (developed competence), and the **1024-dimensional lattice**
(immediate situated cognition).

## Infancy configurations, not permanent ceilings

The dimensionalities 11 / 666 / 1024 are **infancy-stage reference
configurations**. They are where a Rheo organism *starts*, not a ceiling on
where it can go. The architecture must support developmental expansion of any
geometry without arbitrarily freezing it at its initial dimensions.

This is not a new invention of the 2026-10-10 design discussion. The Cognitive
Metamorphic Primer v3 (§14) already states the principle for the 1024-D
reference geometry:

> "A 1024-dimensional reference HSL has been used and verified in bounded
> implementation work… That number is important as a reference geometry. It
> should not automatically become a universal law of all future Rheo
> realizations."

The same discipline applies to all three geometries: reference numbers are
engineering starting points, and any claim that a dimension count is a law of
the architecture requires its own evidence.

## Developmental trajectories

Each geometry develops on its own timescale, and development is directional:

- **1024D (situated):** fastest. Working hypotheses form and dissolve within a
  single episode. Repeated situated patterns consolidate *downward/outward*
  into 666D competence.
- **666D (competence):** intermediate. Consolidation of recurring 1024D
  patterns into stable strategies, anticipatory models, and learned
  dispositions. Exceptional events — surprises, failures, emergencies — can
  restructure competence rapidly (see the taxi example in the architecture
  spec).
- **11D (foundational):** slowest. Enduring dispositions, grounding, role
  representation. Changes here are rare and load-bearing: a shift in 11D
  alters what the organism treats as worth noticing (anticipation) and how it
  acts in the moment.

Influence is bidirectional: foundational dispositions shape anticipatory
cognition and immediate action (11D → 666D → 1024D), while exceptional
situated experience reshapes competence and, over time, foundations
(1024D → 666D → 11D). The nine coupling relationships formalize these paths;
see `../mathematics/nine-coupling-dynamics.md`.

## Growth without rupture

Developmental expansion must preserve **continuity**: a growing organism
should not confuse change with identity loss (Primer v3, §1). Open research
questions:

- What are the structure-preserving embeddings when a geometry gains
  dimensions (RHEO-006)?
- Which invariants must a growth step preserve so that QMT continuity still
  adjudicates the organism as the *same* organism?
- How is growth distinguished from a fork (two organisms where one stood)?

See `../mathematics/dimensional-growth.md` for the formal research scaffold.

## What development is not

- Development is not training in the machine-learning sense of fitting
  parameters to a dataset. It is the organism's own trajectory through
  experience, adjudicated by its constitutional bounds.
- Maturation of competence (666D) must not silently rewrite constitutional
  requirements. See `constitutional-identity-boundaries.md`: enduring
  preferences are not immutable obligations.
