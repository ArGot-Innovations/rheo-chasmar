## Development, specialization, continuity

Experiences accumulate and can produce durable expertise across contexts. Identity is **optional** for general organisms. Application-specific constitutional obligations (e.g. security protection) may require G₀, but G₀ must not be equated with every 11D preference or foundational response. Separate knowledge, skill, preference, biography, current state, identity invariants, and QMT continuity adjudication.

Substrate migration (e.g. workstation → drone) can change sensory affordances, timing, numeric precision and experiential trajectory. Model endogenous divergence between otherwise similarly initialized organisms, including deterministic/stochastic regimes and perturbation sensitivity, **without claiming that physical separation alone mathematically guarantees divergence**. Distinguish cognitive trajectory divergence from a formal identity fork.
