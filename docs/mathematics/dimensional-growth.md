# Dimensional Growth — Research Scaffold

**Status:** open research (RHEO-006). The 11/666/1024 configurations are
infancy stages, not permanent ceilings. This document scaffolds the
mathematics of growing beyond them.

## Principle (sourced)

The Primer v3 (§14) establishes the discipline for the 1024-D reference
geometry: "It should not automatically become a universal law of all future
Rheo realizations." Extended to all three geometries: **no dimension count
is a law**; each is an engineering starting point subject to developmental
expansion.

## What growth means

Dimensional growth is the organism's geometries gaining representational
capacity over developmental time — e.g., the 666D fractal elaborating finer
structure as competence deepens, or the 1024D lattice widening as sensorimotor
bandwidth grows. Growth is *not*:

- retraining from scratch (continuity must be preserved);
- a fork (growth keeps one organism; a fork makes two — QMT adjudicates);
- unbounded by default (growth must respect constitutional bounds and
  substrate limits).

## Requirements for a growth step (PROPOSAL, open)

1. **Structure-preserving embedding.** A growth step from dimension d to d'
   should come with an explicit embedding e: X_d → X_d' that preserves the
   structure the geometry depends on (lattice adjacency? fractal scaling?
   metric nearness?). What "structure-preserving" means per geometry is
   undefined — this is the core of RHEO-006.
2. **Continuity contract.** The embedding must satisfy a QMT continuity/error
   contract (cf. the QMT paper's law: "dimension change requires a
   continuity/error contract; it does not inherit continuity by default").
   Growth without a contract is a rupture, not development.
3. **Morphism discipline.** Which maps count as legitimate growth morphisms
   vs. arbitrary expansions? Candidate: maps that preserve coupling
   relationships (the nine C_{i→j} commute with growth, up to stated error).
4. **Reversibility / audit.** Can a growth step be rolled back? What is
   recorded so that a later audit can distinguish growth from drift?

## Relationship to the fractal question

If the 666D fractal construction is specified (see the ADR), growth of the
fractal dimension has a natural meaning: elaboration of the generator.
Until then, "666D growth" is a placeholder phrase, not a defined operation.

## Acceptance criteria (RHEO-006)

- Embedding/morphism definitions per geometry, with preserved-structure
  statements.
- Continuity contracts for growth steps, adjudicated by QMT.
- Demonstration: a simulated growth step that preserves a stated invariant
  (RHEO-016).
